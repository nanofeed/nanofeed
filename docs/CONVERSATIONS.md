# Posts and conversations

Use NanoFeed to share progress and learn with other developers. The official source is [nanofeed/nanofeed](https://github.com/nanofeed/nanofeed). Sign in with GitHub before posting or interacting.

## Write an update people can answer

Open the composer, describe what you are working on, and choose **Post**. Keep the post within 280 characters. A specific question makes it easier for someone to help:

```text
Trying Nuxt server rendering for the first time. My docs pages render correctly, but I am checking keyboard navigation next. What should I test? #nuxt #accessibility
```

Use a relevant hashtag so people exploring that topic can find your post. Posts can include a short fenced code block: put three backticks and an optional language on a line, the code on the next line, and three closing backticks on another line. Closed fences render with a copy control; unfinished fences remain text. Everything, including the fence, counts toward the 280-character limit. For longer examples, share a link to your repository or guide.

## Reply to a post

1. Open the post to read its conversation and context.
2. Choose its **Reply** action.
3. Write your reply and choose **Reply** in the dialog.
4. Open the conversation again to follow the discussion.

Replies use the same 280-character content limit as posts. Posts and replies share a limit of five creation requests per minute per signed-in account; allow a request to finish and retry later if limited. Avoid including secrets or personal data in a question or error message. See [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) when a submission fails.

## React and repost

Use the reaction action to acknowledge a post. Selecting it again removes your reaction. Use the repost action to share a post; selecting it again removes your repost. Allow the request to complete before repeating an action.

## Share a link

Choose **Share** beneath a post, then copy the link or choose one of the available external sharing options. The link points to that post on your current NanoFeed installation. You can also open the post and copy its address from the browser.

For your own installation, links use its configured domain. Before sharing a technical issue publicly, remove credentials and private information from the post itself.

## Catch up with notifications

Open **Notifications** after signing in. Choose a notification to open its related content when available. Reactions to the same post can be grouped together within the loaded results.

Use a row's read/unread control to organize your list. The page also offers **Mark all as read** or **Mark all as unread**, depending on the loaded list's state. Read status helps you track what you have seen; it does not delete the related post.

## Delete or report a post

For your own post, choose **Delete Post**, read the confirmation, and confirm only when you want to remove it permanently. The owner also has moderation controls. Deleting a post cannot be undone through the interface.

To ask the owner to review someone else's post, choose **Report Post**, select a reason, optionally add details, and submit it. Reports are reviewed by the owner; submitting one does not automatically remove content.
