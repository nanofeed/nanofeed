# Connect with developers

Connections on NanoFeed start with a profile, a follow, or a helpful reply. The official project repository is [nanofeed/nanofeed](https://github.com/nanofeed/nanofeed).

## Make yourself easy to recognize

Sign in with GitHub, then open **Settings → Profile**. Add a recognizable display name, avatar, and a short bio describing what you build or want to learn. Choose an available username you are comfortable sharing. See [Using NanoFeed](USING-NANOFEED.md) for profile limits and your first post.

For example, your bio could say:

```text
Building small web apps with Vue. Learning PostgreSQL. Happy to discuss accessibility and open source.
```

## Find people and topics

1. Open **Search** and enter a person's name, username, a topic, or a phrase from a post.
2. Use the search tabs to focus the results. Open a person's profile to see their work.
3. Select a hashtag on a post to browse related posts, such as `#nuxt` or `#buildinpublic`.
4. Explore **Latest** for recent posts and **For you** for recommendations. A recommendation's explanation helps you understand why it appeared.

An empty result does not mean a person has an account under their GitHub username. Users can choose a different NanoFeed username. Ask for their profile link if needed. Profiles can display owner, verified developer, and verified contributor badges together; see [Account and privacy](ACCOUNT.md) for what each means.

## Follow someone

Open their profile and choose **Follow**. The control changes to **Unfollow** after a successful request. Following is a one-way connection; the other person can decide whom they want to follow separately.

Open the **Following** feed to read posts from people you follow. You can open the **Followers** and **Following** counts on a profile to browse those lists. When signed in, the list also provides follow controls for other accounts.

To stop following someone, choose **Unfollow** on their profile or in a follow list. Check the displayed state before clicking again, and allow a request to finish.

## Start a conversation

Read a post, choose **Reply**, and add something specific: a question, a useful reference, or a detail from your own experience. For example:

```text
Nice progress on the migration! How did you check that existing profiles kept their IDs?
```

Use reactions to acknowledge a post and reposts to share it with your circle. See [Posts and conversations](CONVERSATIONS.md) for the steps and notification controls.

## Share your profile

Open your profile and copy its address from the browser. Profile addresses use your current username:

```text
https://nanofeed.vercel.app/profile/YOUR_USERNAME
```

Replace `YOUR_USERNAME` with your NanoFeed username. Update shared links if you later change that username.

## Keep connections useful

Give context when asking for help, acknowledge people's time, and avoid repeated promotional replies. Report a post through its report action when it needs owner review. Read [Account and privacy](ACCOUNT.md) before sharing personal information.

If following or search fails, check [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md). Product suggestions belong in [the official issue tracker](https://github.com/nanofeed/nanofeed/issues).
